### 🚗 Xiaomi in China’s EV Market (2025)

Xiaomi entered the electric vehicle market in 2024 with the SU7 — a tech-focused electric sedan. Despite being a newcomer, it made an explosive debut, driven by Xiaomi’s strong ecosystem and fanbase.

---

#### 📈 Summary of Xiaomi’s Position

| Factor               | Standing                                          |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------|

---

#### 🧠 Strategic Advantage

- Software-first car experience: **HyperOS** across phones, wearables, and vehicles
- Strong loyalty and ecosystem lock-in
- “Apple Car-like” experience before Apple even launches one

---

#### 🏁 Outlook

Xiaomi is not yet a volume leader, 
but is already a **top-5 contender in mindshare**. 
If it scales production and maintains quality, 
it could disrupt both tech and auto incumbents in China’s booming EV space.

### 👤 Xiaomi Management & Ownership Overview (2025)

---

#### 🧑‍💼 Founder & CEO: Lei Jun

- **Role:** Co-founder, Chairman, and CEO
- **Background:**
    - Often called the "Steve Jobs of China"
    - Founded **Joyo.com** (sold to Amazon in 2004)
    - Launched Xiaomi in 2010
    - Personally led Xiaomi’s EV entry (SU7 launch)

- **Style:**
    - Visionary, product-centric
    - Deeply involved in strategy and marketing
    - Famous quote: *“We don’t sell products — we sell innovation and trust.”*

---

#### 🧑‍💼 Key Management Team

| Name              | Role                              | Notable Facts                                           |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| **Lu Weibing**     | President of Xiaomi Group         | Leads smartphones and international expansion            |
| **Zeng Xuezhong**  | SVP, Head of Smartphone Dept      | Ex-ZTE exec, heads R&D and product strategy              |
| **Li Xiaoshuang**  | GM, Xiaomi Auto                   | Oversees EV operations and SU7 production                |
| **Chen Tong**      | Chief Brand Officer               | Brand strategy, PR, launch storytelling                  |
| **Wang Xiang**     | Former President (retired 2023)   | Led IPO and early global expansion                       |

---

#### 🏦 Ownership Structure

- **Listed on:** Hong Kong Stock Exchange (HKEX: 1810.HK)
- **Founder Control:**
    - **Lei Jun** holds ~25–30% voting power via **dual-class shares**
- **Institutional Investors:**
    - Includes **BlackRock**, **Vanguard**, and others
- **Retail Investors:**
    - Broad base, strong in Asia, especially mainland China

**Note:**  
Xiaomi uses a **dual-class share structure**, so Lei Jun retains **strategic control** despite lower economic ownership.

---

#### 🧬 Company Culture & Strategy

- **Flat hierarchy:** Empowered engineering/product teams
- **Product-obsessed:** Similar to Apple; driven by refinement and user feedback
- **Ecosystem vision:** Phones, IoT, EVs — all integrated under **HyperOS**

---

#### 🧾 TL;DR

- **Lei Jun** is the visionary founder and public face of Xiaomi.
- The **management team** is deeply experienced in tech, with a focus on expansion and innovation.
- **Ownership is public**, but Lei Jun holds long-term control.
- Xiaomi operates more like a **tech innovator** than a traditional hardware company — key to its smartphone and EV strategies.
